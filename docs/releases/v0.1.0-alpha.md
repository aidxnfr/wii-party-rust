v0.1.0-alpha - First release: a launcher that builds Wii Party from your own disc and plays it as a native Rust program.
The first install builds everything (about an hour and a half on the PC it was tested on); later updates only rebuild what changed.

## Getting started

1. Download **wii-party-launcher.exe** below and run it. Nothing to install first: missing tools (Rust, MinGW, Python, SDL3, nodtool) are downloaded from their official sites into the launcher's own folder.
2. On the **Game** page, press **Choose** and pick your Wii Party disc image (`.iso`, `.wbfs`, `.rvz`, `.wia`, `.ciso` or `.gcm`), dumped from your own copy.
3. Press **Install**. Needs about 25 GB of free space.
4. Press **Play**.

Your saves and settings live in `data` inside the install folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\WiiPartyRust` by default) and are never touched by installs or updates.

## What's in this release

* **The game, translated to Rust.** Your disc is recompiled with arelkair's [wiiparty-recomp](https://github.com/arelkair/wiiparty-recomp) and its output is translated to Rust (22 main-program units, 115 modules, the audio DSP driver). The runtime, renderer, input, audio and saves are Rust; the window and graphics run on Bevy and wgpu (DX12).
* **Checked against the original.** 23 recorded play sessions (boot, menus, Board Game Island, minigames, results) are replayed and compared frame by frame; all 19,802 frames match.
* **Faster.** Game thread down from 15.6 s to 9.1 s of CPU per minute of play (about 36 s in total across all threads, from 43.8 s), with an idle nap between frames and builds tuned for modern CPUs (x86-64-v3).
* **Launcher.** Sidebar with Game, Settings, Controls and Licenses pages; dark and light themes; borderless window. The design follows arelkair's launcher.
* **Install progress.** Overall and per-step percentages, a time-left estimate that learns how long each step takes on your PC, step-by-step checklist, full log under Show details, and Cancel.
* **Settings.** Smooth motion (2x, 3x or 4x in-between frames for high refresh rate screens; off by default), performance window, compile jobs, offline mode, install folder, saves folder.
* **Performance window.** FPS, CPU per thread and memory of the running game, plus the latest game log lines.
* **Regions.** USA (SUPE01) and Europe (SUPP01) discs. Japanese and Korean discs are untested.
* **Updates.** The launcher keeps your recompiled game code and only rebuilds what a new version changes.

## Known limits

* Windows 10/11, 64-bit only.
* Real Wii Remotes are off; keyboard, mouse and gamepads work. Bindings are in the game options (F10).
* Smooth motion can show small glitches and adds a little delay; it stays off unless you turn it on.
* This is an alpha: reports are welcome.
